Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

The short version

Selena Roller is a game made by Ozhan Duyan. It does not collect any personal data.

  • No accounts, sign-in or sign-up.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • Your progress is saved only on your own device.
  • The iPhone app makes no network requests. It works without an internet connection.
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the game keeps on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small record on your device: how many stars you have, the levels you have reached, the balls you have unlocked and picked, your speed setting, whether sound is on, and whether the steering tip has been shown.

This record stays on your device. It is not sent anywhere, it is not linked to you, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

The iPhone app

Everything the app needs is built into it. It does not connect to the internet, and it contains no code from advertising, analytics or tracking companies. On the App Store its privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Apple runs the App Store and iOS under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage figures. We use these only to find and fix problems.

The web version

You can also play Selena Roller in a web browser. That version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical logs of visits, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs.

The web version uses its own copy of its lettering (the Rubik font), served by GitHub Pages with the rest of the game, so your browser does not contact Google or any other company for it.

Your browser keeps your progress, and a copy of the game files so it can play without a signal, in storage for this website on your device. Clearing your browser's website data removes it.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you, and we delete the conversation if you ask us to.

Children

Selena Roller does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no ads, no chat, no accounts and no in-app purchases. The balls in the game are unlocked with stars collected while playing.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
